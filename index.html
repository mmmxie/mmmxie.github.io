AI · Content Automation2024
AI-Powered SEO
Content Automation Pipeline
Built an end-to-end automation that scaled editorial output 7× by combining Google Trends data with a fine-tuned internal ML model.
+
Problem

JUKSY's SEO content team was producing just 3 articles per editor per day — manually sourcing trends, drafting copy, and getting approvals. Growth targets required significantly higher output without proportional headcount growth.

Solution

Designed and shipped an AI content pipeline that automated the full ideation-to-draft cycle. The system aggregated trending topics from Google Trends and other sources, passed them through an internal ML model trained on JUKSY's brand voice and editorial style, and produced near-publish-ready drafts for editors to review and finalise.

Google Trends API→ Topic aggregation→ Internal ML model→ Draft generation→ Editor review→ CMS publish
My Contributions

Defined the product requirements and success metrics. Collaborated with data science to scope the ML model training dataset and evaluation criteria. Built the integration layer connecting Google Trends API, the internal model, and the editorial CMS. Ran A/B testing to validate output quality before full rollout.

Outcome

The pipeline reduced time-to-draft by ~85%, allowing editors to focus on quality control rather than topic research and blank-page writing. The team's SEO performance improved measurably within 60 days of launch.
